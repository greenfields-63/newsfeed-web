Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Information stored in your browser

Newsfeed uses browser local storage to remember your selected news sources, layout, language and display preferences, cached headlines, and headline translation cache. The site’s service worker can cache same-site files so the site can load more reliably. This information is stored in your browser; you can clear it using your browser’s site-data controls. Disabling storage or service workers may affect site features.

Requests to Newsfeed and service providers

When you visit or use the site, your browser makes requests to deliver the pages and features you use. The site is hosted using Cloudflare Pages and obtains news snapshots from its API. It also requests the source list from GitHub Gist and commentary, when available, from a service hosted on Render. These providers process request information, such as your IP address and technical browser data, as needed to deliver and secure their services and under their own privacy terms.

Google services, advertising, and translation

The site loads Google AdSense code. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure, and personalize advertising, subject to your location, consent choices, and Google’s policies. You can learn about Google’s use of information at Google’s advertising information page and manage available ad personalization settings in your Google Ads Settings.

If you choose headline translation, the headline text and language selections are sent to Google’s translation service to produce the requested translation. The site also loads Google Fonts and the Tailwind CSS content delivery network to display its pages. Those services may receive technical request information directly from your browser.

Links to other sites

News headlines link to third-party publishers. When you follow an external link, that publisher’s privacy practices apply. Review the destination site’s privacy policy before providing it with personal information.

Your choices and questions

You can clear browser storage, change your Newsfeed preferences, and use available Google ad settings or browser controls for cookies. For story corrections, contact the publisher through the linked article. See our Contact page or email sethssignals@gmail.com for site-related information.
