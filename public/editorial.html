Editorial Standards

Sources and selection

Newsfeed selects news sources and displays headlines supplied by their feeds. Source availability and update frequency vary; a headline list is not a complete record of the news or a guarantee that every item has been independently verified by Newsfeed.

Attribution and links

Headlines are shown with the source name and link to the original publisher when a valid article link is available. The publisher is responsible for the article’s full text, reporting, updates, and corrections. Please use the linked article to read the publisher’s complete coverage.

Translations

Readers may request machine-translated headlines. Automated translations can be inaccurate or miss context; compare with the original headline and linked article when accuracy matters.

Commentary

“Seth’s Signals,” when available, is independent commentary and is distinct from reporting by linked news organizations. Commentary should not be mistaken for a publisher’s reporting or endorsement.
